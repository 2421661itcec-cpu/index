PharmaGuard 2.0

Pharmaceutical supply-chain disruption management

When a flood closes a distribution hub, medicines get stuck. PharmaGuard helps an operations team record the disruption, see which shipments are affected, and approve a recovery plan from one screen.

Parivartan-2026 · Chandigarh Engineering College-CGC Landran, Mohali · Department of Information Technology · Built by Ankit

How it works

  1. Describe the disruptionThe operator types what happened, for example “Ambala distribution hub is closed due to flooding”.
  2. See the impactThe system shows how severe it is and which shipments and destinations are affected.
  3. Review recovery optionsThe agents propose alternatives such as a different route.
  4. Approve, modify or rejectA supply-chain manager makes the final call before anything runs.
  5. Recovery runsThe screen shows the new route, the travel time and the inventory transferred.
  6. Track incidentsEvery SOS report is stored with an ID, location, category, severity, time and status.

The multi-agent workflow

Seven stages run in order. The amber stage is where a person decides.

  • Disruption sensing
  • Impact analysis
  • Scenario planning
  • Human decision
  • Rerouting
  • Inventory rebalancing
  • Recovery

Human in the loop

PharmaGuard never acts on its own. The manager can approve, modify or reject the proposed plan, and only then are the route change and inventory transfer executed.

SOS incident register

A separate register keeps every emergency report in one list. Users can filter by status (all, open, in progress, resolved), update a record, and turn on desktop notifications for new alerts.

Where it can be used

  • Pharmaceutical manufacturing and distribution
  • Medicine transport and logistics coordination
  • Monitoring disruptions that could delay time-sensitive medicines
  • Incident review, escalation and recovery planning
  • Inventory redistribution and alternate-route planning

PharmaGuard 2.0 is a working prototype shown running on a local computer. It does not yet connect to live data feeds or real emergency services.
